What is WEDORA?

WEDORA is a wedding planning app made for couples in Sri Lanka. Instead of a spreadsheet for the budget, a notebook for the guests and a phone gallery full of vendor quotes, you keep the whole wedding in one plan that you and your partner share.

  • Tasks A checklist that follows your wedding date, from booking the hall to the homecoming, so nothing is remembered too late.
  • Budget Set a total in rupees, split it by category, and log every advance so the balance is always the real one.
  • Guests RSVPs, phone numbers, table groups and a final count you can hand to the hall without rewriting it.
  • Vendors Photographers, dancers, salons and decorators with their quotes and contract dates beside each name.

Creating an account is free and takes about a minute. Your plan stays private to the people you invite into it.
